How Bridge Rescue works

1. Detect

We read the source-chain transaction and classify it as FAILED, STUCK, PENDING, or COMPLETED using public RPC — no API keys needed.

2. Resolve

We identify the exact bridge protocol and the correct recovery path: destination-chain claim, retry, or source-chain refund.

3. Recover

You get a plain-English action plus the evidence, so you can claim or retry with confidence — and get your funds back.
